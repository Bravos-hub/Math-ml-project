# Ethics and Data Governance Statement

## Current study classification

The authoritative analysis uses secondary, aggregate agricultural and environmental data. It recruits no participants, performs no intervention, makes no farmer-level decisions, and contains no direct personal identifiers in the final analytical table. Included AAS DDI XML files are metadata/codebooks, not household microdata.

## Current safeguards

- Public analysis is restricted to aggregate, matched-geography observations.
- Dataset contracts reject proxy, synthetic, geographically assigned, mixed-granularity, duplicate-key, and target-derived predictor data.
- Outputs are retrospective research estimates, not advice, early warnings, insurance/credit decisions, or causal agronomic recommendations.
- Geographic and agronomic equity is assessed through crop-, season-, and subregion-level errors.

## Mandatory re-review triggers

Institutional and governance re-review is required before acquiring or ingesting household/person-level microdata, increasing geographic precision, linking records, adding indirect identifiers, changing the permitted purpose, sharing restricted data, or producing prescriptive fertilizer/pesticide recommendations.

Before microdata enters a controlled environment, document provider authorization, use restrictions, REC/IRB status, de-identification and geographic disclosure assessment, least-privilege access, encryption, audit logging, retention/deletion dates, incident response, publication review, and redistribution prohibitions. Microdata must not be committed to this public repository.
